Climb one habit at a time.

Gradatim is a coaching app for League of Legends players. After every ranked game it tells you, with numbers, what cost you the game, and it keeps you on one improvement focus until it sticks. You judge your sessions on the habits you track, not on your win count.

What it does

A factual report after each game

Avoidable deaths, fights taken outnumbered, side-lane deaths without vision, absences from major objectives, lane leads that were lost, your build and skill order. Every finding comes with the minute it happened, so you can check it in the replay.

One focus at a time

You pick one habit to fix, for example “no solo death in lane”. Gradatim tracks it game after game and shows your progress towards a goal, then moves you to the next one.

An AI coach that reads your games

A text coach writes a short review of each game and answers your questions, grounded in the facts of that game. It is clearly labelled as an AI (Claude, by Anthropic) and is reserved for players aged 18 and over.

A stop-loss that advises

After a run of losses or a chain of deaths, Gradatim suggests a break or the end of the session. It only advises: it never blocks the client or a queue, and never plays or decides for you.

Weekly recaps

Every week (with the subscription), a recap of your focus, your sessions and your stats, compared with your own start rather than with other players.

Nothing during the game

Gradatim shows nothing during champion select or while you play, and gives no information you could not see yourself. Everything happens before and after your games.

Data & privacy

Where the game data comes from

  • Riot Games API: ACCOUNT-V1 (find your account from your Riot ID), MATCH-V5 (your ranked games and their timelines), LEAGUE-V4 (your rank and LP). Only the games of the player using the app.
  • The League client on your PC, read only: your logged-in account, your rank, your recent games. Gradatim never sends anything to the client.
  • The game's live data on your PC (Live Client Data API), read only, saved during your own game for the post-game report. Nothing is shown while you play.

What we keep

  • Your games and reports stay in the app's folder on your PC.
  • In the released app, Riot API calls go through our server: the API key is never shipped in the app.
  • The AI coach receives the facts of the game it reviews, without your name, Riot ID or the other players' names.
  • Deleting your account will delete the game data we hold for it.

Beta & pricing

Closed beta, French-speaking first

Gradatim is in a closed beta with a small group of French-speaking players, invited through the Spotted Bush community. The public release will follow.

Access is by invitation for now.

Free, with an optional subscription

Everything the app computes on your PC is free and unlimited: game reports, stats, focus tracking, the briefing and the stop-loss. The free plan also includes three AI coach reviews a week.

An optional subscription (around €10 a month) adds the coach's review after every game, the conversation with the coach and the weekly recaps. No ads, ever. Beta testers get the full coach for free.
